Workout Performance Privacy

Privacy by architecture

Your workout data stays with you.

Cindy Workout is a static application. It has no user accounts, application backend, database, advertising, or analytics.

01

Local storage only

Completed sessions and your preferred rep recipe are stored in your browser's localStorage. The app never sends that performance history to an application server.

02

No tracking

There are no analytics scripts, cookies, account identifiers, ad networks, or third-party fonts. GitHub Pages only serves the static files required to run the app. Like any web host, GitHub may process ordinary request metadata under its own privacy policy, but it never receives the workout history stored by this app.

03

Local music

The default tracks are included with the application. A personal MP3 selected from your device is represented by a temporary browser URL and is not uploaded by this app.

04

You control deletion

Export or import your history as JSON, clear performance history from the Performance page, erase all app data below, or clear site data in browser settings. JSON files are read directly by your browser and are never uploaded by this app.

No microphone access

Cindy Workout never requests microphone permission and does not listen to or record audio. Fixed coach announcements use local MP3 files; dynamic round and score announcements use text-to-speech output only.

Exactly what is saved

  • Workout completion date and time
  • Completed rounds and calculated rep totals
  • The pull-up, push-up, and squat recipe used
  • Round timestamps and whether a round was logged by button or keyboard
  • Local preferences such as volume, wake lock, and rep recipe

Performance levels are derived in your browser from the saved rounds and recipe. The category is not stored as additional data.
